About Pihu Gupta

Pihu Gupta, known as "Duggar Di Kuddi," is an international cultural dancer and model from Udhampur, Jammu and Kashmir. At just 10 years old, she began her journey to become a cultural ambassador for Dogra heritage, performing traditional dance forms across India and internationally.

  • Date of Birth: 22 March 2013
  • Location: Udhampur, Jammu and Kashmir
  • Nickname: "Duggar Di Kuddi" (Daughter of Dogra Land)
  • Specialization: Dogra Cultural Dance
  • Mentor & Icon: Youth on Beat Dance and Modeling Academy
  • Organization: Youth on Beat Organisation
